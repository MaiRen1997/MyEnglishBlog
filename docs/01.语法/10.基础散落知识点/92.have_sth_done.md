## have_sth_done

1. 请人做某事

   I’m going to `have` my hair `cut` this weekend.

   I’m going to `have` my car `repaired` tomorrow.

2. 某人遭遇某事(多是不好的事)

   I `had` my watch `stolen`.

   King Charles I `had` his head `cut off`.
