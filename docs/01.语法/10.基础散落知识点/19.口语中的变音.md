## 口语中的变音

### t + j -> tʃ

1. last year
2. don’t you
3. won’t you
4. statue
5. situation

### s + j -> ʃ

1. this year
2. sexual
3. Bless you.
4. I promise you.

### d + j  ->  dʒ

1. would you
2. should you
3. I need your help.

### z + j = ʒ

1. use your book
2. How’s your father
