List1-Unit-1

# 1. abandon

释义 vt. 离弃，放弃（desert） n. 放纵，放任 例句 (vt. 离弃) The bad weather forced them to abandon their plan to climb the mountain. 恶劣的天气迫使他们放弃了登山计划。 (vt. 放弃) She decided to abandon her career in law and pursue her passion for art. 她决定放弃法律事业，追求她对艺术的热爱。 (n. 放任) The children were playing with wild abandon in the playground. 孩子们在操场上尽情地玩耍。

# 2. insane

释义 a. 蠢极的，荒唐的；（患）精神病的，精神失常的，疯狂的 例句 (a. 荒唐的) It would be insane to drive all the way across the country without stopping for a rest. 一路开车横穿全国而不停下来休息是极其荒唐的。 (a. 精神失常的) The psychiatrist testified that the defendant was clinically insane at the time of the crime. 精神科医生作证称，被告在犯罪时处于临床精神错乱状态。

# 3. barter

释义 n.&v. 物品交换，实物交易 例句 (n. 物品交换) Barter was the primary form of trade long before currency was invented. 早在货币发明之前，物物交换就是主要的贸易形式。 (v. 实物交易) In rural areas, farmers still barter their crops for tools and other necessities. 在农村地区，农民仍然用他们的农作物换取工具和其他必需品。

# 4. insert

释义 v. 插入，嵌入 例句 (v. 插入) Please insert your bank card into the ATM and enter your PIN. 请将您的银行卡插入自动取款机并输入密码。 (v. 嵌入) The editor decided to insert a new paragraph into the article to clarify the argument. 编辑决定在文章中嵌入一个新的段落来阐明论点。

# 5. abolish

释义 vt. 废止，废除（法律、制度、习俗等）（exterminate） 例句 (vt. 废除) Many countries have abolished the death penalty in recent decades. 近几十年来，许多国家已经废除了死刑。 (vt. 废止) The new government promised to abolish all forms of racial discrimination. 新政府承诺废止一切形式的种族歧视。

# 6. compliment

释义 vt.&n. 赞美（praise, commendation） 【派】complimentary 例句 (vt. 赞美) The professor complimented the student on his well-structured research paper. 教授称赞那位学生的研究论文结构良好。 (n. 赞美) She received many compliments on her excellent presentation at the conference. 她在会议上的精彩演讲赢得了许多赞美。

# 7. perimeter

释义 n. 周长；周边 例句 (n. 周长) Calculate the perimeter of the rectangle by adding all four sides together. 把四条边加起来计算矩形的周长。 (n. 周边) Security guards patrol the perimeter of the building every two hours. 保安每两小时在大楼周边巡逻一次。

# 8. sedate

释义 a. 安静的（calm, composed） 例句 (a. 安静的) The old man walked at a sedate pace, enjoying the afternoon sunshine. 老人以不慌不忙的步伐走着，享受着午后的阳光。 (a. 镇静的) The doctor recommended a sedate lifestyle for the patient with high blood pressure. 医生建议那位高血压患者过一种平静的生活方式。

# 9. famine

释义 n. 饥荒；饥饿；严重的缺乏 例句 (n. 饥荒) The prolonged drought led to a severe famine that affected millions of people. 持久的干旱导致了一场严重的饥荒，影响了数百万人。 (n. 严重缺乏) The country is experiencing a water famine after months without rainfall. 该国在数月无雨后正经历严重的水资源短缺。

# 10. relentless

释义 a. 无情的，残酷的；不断的；不懈的 例句 (a. 不断的) The team faced relentless pressure from the media throughout the competition. 整个比赛期间，该团队都面临着来自媒体的持续压力。 (a. 不懈的) Her relentless pursuit of excellence finally earned her a scholarship to Cambridge. 她对卓越的不懈追求最终为她赢得了剑桥大学的奖学金。

# 11. retort

释义 v. 反驳，反击；回报 例句 (v. 反驳) When accused of being lazy, he retorted that he had been working harder than anyone else. 当被指责懒惰时，他反驳说自己比任何人都更努力地工作。 (v. 回报) She retorted his insults with a brilliant speech that silenced everyone. 她用一场精彩的演讲回击了他的侮辱，让所有人哑口无言。

# 12. sympathy

释义 n. 同情；赞同，支持 例句 (n. 同情) I expressed my sympathy for the victims of the natural disaster. 我对自然灾害的受害者表示了同情。 (n. 支持) The workers went on strike in sympathy with their colleagues who had been unfairly dismissed. 工人们举行罢工以支持被不公平解雇的同事。

# 13. accent

释义 n. 重音；口音 例句 (n. 口音) Although he has lived in London for ten years, he still speaks with a strong French accent. 尽管他在伦敦住了十年，他说话仍然带有浓重的法国口音。 (n. 重音) In the word "important", the accent falls on the second syllable. 在"important"这个词中，重音在第二个音节上。

# 14. deliberate

释义 a. 故意的；深思熟虑的；从容不迫的 v. 深思熟虑；审议 例句 (a. 故意的) The damage to the car was not accidental but a deliberate act of vandalism. 对汽车的损坏不是意外的，而是故意的破坏行为。 (a. 从容不迫的) She walked into the room with deliberate steps, commanding everyone's attention. 她以从容不迫的步伐走进房间，吸引了所有人的注意。 (v. 审议) The jury deliberated for three days before reaching a verdict. 陪审团审议了三天才做出裁决。

# 15. widespread

释义 a. 分布（或散布）广的；普遍的 例句 (a. 普遍的) The widespread use of smartphones has changed the way people communicate. 智能手机的普遍使用改变了人们的交流方式。 (a. 分布广的) The disease is widespread in tropical regions of the world. 这种疾病在世界热带地区分布很广。

# 16. provided

释义 conj. 倘若，只要，假如 例句 (conj. 只要) I will accept the job offer provided that the salary meets my expectations. 只要薪水符合我的期望，我就会接受这份工作。 (conj. 倘若) You can go to the party provided you finish your homework first. 倘若你先完成作业，你就可以去参加聚会。

# 17. grouchy

释义 a. 不悦的，愠怒的（bad-tempered, petulant） 例句 (a. 愠怒的) He is always grouchy in the morning before he has his first cup of coffee. 他在早上喝第一杯咖啡之前总是愠怒的。 (a. 不悦的) The old man gave a grouchy response to the children's noisy laughter. 老人对孩子们吵闹的笑声给出了不悦的回应。

# 18. orthodox

释义 a. 正统的；传统的；习惯的；东正教的 例句 (a. 正统的) The orthodox view of economics has been challenged by new theories in recent years. 正统的经济学观点近年来受到了新理论的挑战。 (a. 传统的) She follows an orthodox diet based on the traditional cuisine of her hometown. 她遵循一种基于家乡传统饮食的正统饮食方式。

# 19. transmute

释义 v. 改变；（使）变化，变形，变质 例句 (v. 使变化) Alchemists once tried to transmute ordinary metals into gold. 炼金术士曾试图将普通金属变成黄金。 (v. 改变) The intense heat can transmute the rock into a completely different substance. 高温可以使岩石变成一种完全不同的物质。

# 20. specialize

释义 v. 专门研究，专攻；专用于，使适应特殊目的 【派】specialization 例句 (v. 专攻) He decided to specialize in international law after graduating from law school. 从法学院毕业后，他决定专攻国际法。 (v. 专门研究) Many doctors specialize in a particular area of medicine. 许多医生专门研究医学的某个特定领域。

# 21. reform

释义 n.&v. 改革；改善；革新 【派】reformation 例句 (n. 改革) The education system is in urgent need of comprehensive reform. 教育体系迫切需要全面的改革。 (v. 改革) The government has promised to reform the healthcare system to provide better services. 政府承诺改革医疗体系以提供更好的服务。

# 22. dizzy

释义 a. 头晕目眩的，眩晕的；（使）人头晕的 例句 (a. 头晕目眩的) She felt dizzy after spinning around on the playground for too long. 在操场上转圈太久后，她感到头晕目眩。 (a. 使人头晕的) The dizzy height of the skyscraper made him feel nervous. 摩天大楼令人眩晕的高度让他感到紧张。

# 23. gadget

释义 n. 小巧的器械，精巧的装置；小玩意 例句 (n. 精巧的装置) This kitchen gadget makes it easy to peel apples in just a few seconds. 这个厨房小装置可以在几秒钟内轻松削苹果皮。 (n. 小玩意) He loves buying the latest electronic gadgets on the market. 他喜欢购买市场上最新的电子小玩意。

# 24. diffuse

释义 v. 散布，传播；（光等）漫射；（使气体或液体）扩散，弥漫 a. 扩散的，漫射的；（文章等）冗长的，难解的 例句 (v. 散布) The organization works to diffuse knowledge about environmental protection. 该组织致力于传播环境保护知识。 (v. 扩散) The perfume gradually diffused throughout the entire room. 香水逐渐扩散到整个房间。 (a. 冗长的) The professor's diffuse lecture confused many students. 教授冗长的讲座让许多学生感到困惑。

# 25. burst

释义 v.（使）爆炸；突然发作；破裂 n. 爆炸 例句 (v. 突然发作) She burst into tears when she heard the sad news. 当她听到这个悲伤的消息时，她突然哭了起来。 (v. 破裂) The water pipe burst because of the freezing weather. 由于寒冷的天气，水管破裂了。 (n. 爆炸) The burst of laughter from the audience broke the silence. 观众爆发出的笑声打破了寂静。

# 26. abstract

释义 ['æbstrækt] a. 抽象的；抽象派的 n. 摘要，梗概；抽象 [əb'strækt] vt. 摘要，提炼；抽象化 例句 (a. 抽象的) It is often difficult for young children to understand abstract concepts like time. 年幼的孩子通常很难理解像时间这样抽象的概念。 (n. 摘要) Please write an abstract of your research paper before the final submission. 请在最终提交前为你的研究论文写一份摘要。 (vt. 提炼) The scientist managed to abstract a pure compound from the mixture. 科学家设法从混合物中提取出了一种纯化合物。

# 27. drill

释义 n. 钻孔机 v. 训练；钻孔 例句 (v. 训练) The teacher drilled the students in grammar exercises every day. 老师每天训练学生们做语法练习。 (v. 钻孔) The workers need to drill a hole in the wall to install the air conditioner. 工人们需要在墙上钻孔来安装空调。 (n. 钻孔机) He used an electric drill to make holes in the wooden board. 他用电动钻孔机在木板上打孔。

# 28. irrigate

释义 vt. 灌溉；修水利 例句 (vt. 灌溉) Farmers irrigate their crops with water from the nearby river. 农民用附近河里的水灌溉他们的庄稼。 (vt. 修水利) The government plans to irrigate the desert areas to make them suitable for farming. 政府计划在沙漠地区修水利，使其适合耕种。

# 29. scandal

释义 n. 丑事，丑闻；恶意诽谤；流言蜚语；反感，愤慨 例句 (n. 丑闻) The political scandal led to the resignation of several senior officials. 这起政治丑闻导致了几名高级官员的辞职。 (n. 流言蜚语) The scandal about the famous actor spread quickly on social media. 关于那位著名演员的流言蜚语在社交媒体上迅速传播。

# 30. foresight

释义 n. 预见，远见 例句 (n. 远见) Thanks to his foresight, the company had enough resources to survive the economic crisis. 多亏了他的远见，公司有足够的资源度过经济危机。 (n. 预见) A good leader should have the foresight to anticipate future challenges. 一个好的领导者应该有预见未来挑战的远见。

# 31. premiere

释义 n. 首演，首映 例句 (n. 首映) The premiere of the new film attracted many famous celebrities. 这部新电影的首映吸引了许多知名名人。 (n. 首演) The ballet will have its world premiere at the National Theatre next month. 这部芭蕾舞剧将于下个月在国家剧院进行全球首演。

# 32. rigid

释义 a. 死板的；刚硬的；固定的；严格的 例句 (a. 严格的) The school has rigid rules about student uniforms. 学校对学生制服有严格的规定。 (a. 死板的) His rigid attitude towards change made it difficult for the team to innovate. 他对变化的死板态度使团队难以创新。

# 33. solicitous

释义 a. 渴望的（avid）；焦虑的（concerned） 例句 (a. 焦虑的) The mother was solicitous about her son's health after he caught a cold. 儿子感冒后，母亲对他的健康感到焦虑。 (a. 渴望的) She is solicitous for her daughter's success in the entrance examination. 她渴望女儿在入学考试中成功。

# 34. strain

释义 v.&n. 拉紧，绷紧；扭伤，拉伤；（使）过劳，（使）极度紧张（stretch, tense） 例句 (v. 拉紧) The heavy load strained the rope to its breaking point. 重物把绳子拉紧到了断裂的临界点。 (n. 极度紧张) The constant pressure at work has put a great strain on her mental health. 工作中持续不断的压力给她的心理健康带来了极大的紧张。 (v. 扭伤) He strained his back while lifting a heavy box. 他在搬运重箱子时扭伤了背。

# 35. significant

释义 a. 重大的；效果显著的（remarkable） 【派】significance 例句 (a. 重大的) The discovery of a new energy source is a significant breakthrough for science. 新能源的发现对科学来说是一个重大的突破。 (a. 效果显著的) There has been a significant improvement in the city's air quality. 该市的空气质量有了显著的改善。

# 36. onslaught

释义 n. 猛烈的攻击（charge） 例句 (n. 猛烈的攻击) The army launched an onslaught against the enemy's position. 军队对敌人的阵地发动了猛烈的攻击。 (n. 猛烈的攻击) The company faced an onslaught of criticism from the public. 该公司面临来自公众的猛烈批评。

# 37. hasty

释义 a. 匆忙的，草率的 例句 (a. 匆忙的) We had a hasty breakfast before rushing to the airport. 我们匆忙吃了早餐就赶去机场了。 (a. 草率的) Don't make a hasty decision that you might regret later. 不要做草率的决定，以免日后后悔。

# 38. denial

释义 n. 否认，否定；拒绝 例句 (n. 否认) His denial of the accusation only made the situation worse. 他对指控的否认只会让情况变得更糟。 (n. 拒绝) The denial of her visa application came as a huge disappointment. 她签证申请的拒绝让她大为失望。

# 39. tempt

释义 vt. 诱使（lure, entice） 【派】temptation 例句 (vt. 诱使) The advertisement tempted many people to buy the new product. 这则广告诱使许多人购买这款新产品。 (vt. 诱使) The smell of freshly baked bread tempted her into the bakery. 新鲜出炉的面包香味诱使她走进了面包店。

# 40. bigoted

释义 a. 固执己见的（narrow-minded, intolerant） 例句 (a. 固执己见的) His bigoted views on immigration prevented him from seeing the benefits of cultural diversity. 他对移民的固执己见的观点阻碍了他看到文化多样性的好处。 (a. 固执己见的) She is so bigoted that she refuses to listen to anyone else's opinion. 她如此固执己见，以至于拒绝听任何其他人的意见。

# 41. jagged

释义 a. 锯齿状的；（外形）参差不齐的 例句 (a. 锯齿状的) The jagged edges of the broken glass were very dangerous. 碎玻璃的锯齿状边缘非常危险。 (a. 参差不齐的) The mountain range has a jagged skyline that looks beautiful at sunset. 山脉有着参差不齐的天际线，在日落时看起来很美。

# 42. bend

释义 n.&v. 弯曲；折叠；屈服 例句 (v. 弯曲) The strong wind bent the trees almost to the ground. 大风把树吹得几乎弯到了地面。 (n. 弯曲) The river takes a sharp bend near the old castle. 这条河在古堡附近有一个急转弯。 (v. 屈服) He refused to bend to the pressure from his colleagues. 他拒绝向来自同事的压力屈服。

# 43. hire

释义 v.&n. 租用；雇用 例句 (v. 雇用) The company hired a new manager to lead the sales team. 公司雇用了一位新经理来领导销售团队。 (v. 租用) We decided to hire a car for our trip around the island. 我们决定租一辆车环岛旅行。 (n. 租用) The hire of the equipment includes insurance and maintenance. 设备的租用包括保险和维护。

# 44. jostle

释义 v. 推挤；挤开通路 例句 (v. 推挤) People jostled each other to get a better view of the parade. 人们互相推挤，想更好地观看游行队伍。 (v. 挤开通路) He jostled his way through the crowded market. 他挤过拥挤的市场。

# 45. intravenous

释义 a. [医]静脉内的；静脉注射的 例句 (a. 静脉注射的) The patient received intravenous fluids to prevent dehydration. 病人接受了静脉注射液体以防止脱水。 (a. 静脉内的) The nurse inserted an intravenous needle into the patient's arm. 护士将静脉注射针头插入病人的手臂。

# 46. encourage

释义 v. 鼓励；助长 例句 (v. 鼓励) Her teacher always encouraged her to pursue her dreams. 她的老师总是鼓励她追求自己的梦想。 (v. 助长) The warm weather encouraged the growth of plants in the garden. 温暖的天气助长了花园里植物的生长。

# 47. hatred

释义 n. 憎恨，仇恨 例句 (n. 仇恨) He felt deep hatred for the people who had destroyed his family. 他对那些毁了他家庭的人怀有深深的仇恨。 (n. 憎恨) The speech was full of hatred and prejudice. 这次演讲充满了仇恨和偏见。

# 48. flicker

释义 n.&vi. 闪烁；一闪而过 例句 (vi. 闪烁) The candle flickered in the gentle breeze. 蜡烛在微风中闪烁。 (vi. 一闪而过) A smile flickered across her face. 她的脸上闪过一丝微笑。 (n. 闪烁) The flicker of the streetlight made the street look mysterious. 街灯的闪烁让街道看起来神秘。

# 49. overfill

释义 v. 装得太多，装到满溢 例句 (v. 装得太多) Don't overfill the washing machine, or it won't work properly. 不要把洗衣机装得太满，否则它会无法正常工作。 (v. 装到满溢) He overfilled his plate with food and couldn't finish it all. 他把盘子装得太满，无法全部吃完。

# 50. retreat

释义 n.&v. 撤退；退却 例句 (v. 撤退) The army retreated from the battlefield after suffering heavy losses. 军队在遭受重大损失后从战场撤退。 (n. 撤退) The general ordered a strategic retreat to save the remaining forces. 将军下令战略性撤退以保存剩余兵力。

# 51. frowzy

释义 a. 不整洁的（filthy）；臭的 例句 (a. 不整洁的) The frowzy room made her feel uncomfortable. 那个不整洁的房间让她感到不舒服。 (a. 臭的) The frowzy smell from the rubbish bin filled the kitchen. 垃圾桶散发的臭味充满了厨房。

# 52. laboratory

释义 n. 实验室，研究室 例句 (n. 实验室) The scientists spent hours in the laboratory conducting experiments. 科学家们在实验室里花了几个小时进行实验。 (n. 研究室) The university has a state-of-the-art laboratory for medical research. 这所大学有一个最先进的医学研究室。

# 53. dogma

释义 n. 教条，教义，信条 例句 (n. 教条) The political party's dogma left little room for debate. 该政党的教条几乎没有留下辩论的空间。 (n. 信条) He refused to accept the religious dogma of his parents. 他拒绝接受父母宗教信仰的信条。

# 54. discern

释义 v. 目睹；洞悉；辨别（detect, distinguish） 例句 (v. 辨别) It is difficult to discern the difference between the two paintings. 很难辨别这两幅画之间的区别。 (v. 洞悉) She could discern the truth hidden behind his polite words. 她能洞悉隐藏在他礼貌言辞背后的真相。

# 55. arbitrary

释义 a. 任意的；武断的；独裁的，专断的 【派】arbitration 例句 (a. 任意的) The teacher made an arbitrary decision without consulting anyone. 老师在没有咨询任何人的情况下做出了一个任意的决定。 (a. 武断的) His arbitrary judgment of the situation was clearly wrong. 他对局势武断的判断显然错了。

# 56. highlight

释义 vt. 强调，突出；以强烈光线照射；集中注意力于 n. 最精彩的部分，最重要的事件 例句 (vt. 强调) The report highlights the importance of early education. 这份报告强调了早期教育的重要性。 (n. 最精彩的部分) The highlight of our trip was visiting the ancient castle. 我们旅行中最精彩的部分是参观那座古堡。

# 57. charity

释义 n. 施舍（benevolence, altruism）；慈善事业 例句 (n. 慈善事业) The charity provides food and shelter for homeless people. 这家慈善机构为无家可归者提供食物和住所。 (n. 施舍) She donated a large sum of money to charity every year. 她每年向慈善机构捐赠一大笔钱。

# 58. fuzzy

释义 a. 有绒毛的，绒毛状的（frizzy, downy） 例句 (a. 有绒毛的) The fuzzy caterpillar crawled slowly along the leaf. 毛茸茸的毛毛虫沿着树叶慢慢爬行。 (a. 绒毛状的) She wore a fuzzy sweater to keep warm in winter. 她穿了一件绒毛状的毛衣在冬天保暖。

# 59. tough

释义 a. 难对付的；健壮的；（肉等食物）老的 例句 (a. 难对付的) The exam was really tough, and only a few students passed. 这次考试真的很难，只有少数学生通过了。 (a. 健壮的) He is a tough athlete who never gives up. 他是一个从不放弃的健壮运动员。

# 60. robust

释义 a. 健壮的，强壮的；坚定的（sturdy, healthy） 例句 (a. 健壮的) Despite his age, he remains robust and active. 尽管年事已高，他仍然健壮活跃。 (a. 坚定的) She has a robust belief in the importance of education. 她对教育的重要性有着坚定的信念。

# 61. proceed

释义 v. 继续进行；开始，着手 例句 (v. 继续进行) The meeting proceeded despite the technical difficulties. 尽管有技术困难，会议继续进行。 (v. 开始) After the introduction, the speaker proceeded to explain the main topic. 引言之后，演讲者开始解释主要话题。

# 62. excellent

释义 a. 优秀的，杰出的（outstanding, preeminent） 例句 (a. 杰出的) She is an excellent teacher who inspires her students. 她是一位杰出的老师，激励着她的学生。 (a. 优秀的) The restaurant received excellent reviews for its service. 这家餐厅因其服务而获得了优秀的评价。

# 63. extinct

释义 a. 熄灭的；灭绝的；耗尽的 【派】extinction 例句 (a. 灭绝的) Many species have become extinct due to climate change. 由于气候变化，许多物种已经灭绝。 (a. 熄灭的) The volcano has been extinct for thousands of years. 这座火山已经熄灭了数千年。

# 64. mediate

释义 vt. 调停（intercede, intervene） 【派】mediation 例句 (vt. 调停) The government tried to mediate the dispute between the two countries. 政府试图调停两国之间的争端。 (vt. 调停) A professional negotiator was called in to mediate the disagreement. 一位专业谈判人员被请来调停分歧。

# 65. decrepit

释义 a. 破旧的；衰老的 例句 (a. 破旧的) The decrepit building was in danger of collapsing. 这栋破旧的建筑有倒塌的危险。 (a. 衰老的) The decrepit old man could barely walk without assistance. 那位衰老的老人没有帮助几乎无法行走。

# 66. initial

释义 n. 首字母 a. 开始的；最初的；首字母的 例句 (a. 最初的) The initial plan was to finish the project by December. 最初的计划是在12月前完成这个项目。 (n. 首字母) Please write your initials at the bottom of the page. 请在页面底部写上你名字的首字母。

# 67. access

释义 vt. 进入；使用 n. 通道，入径；机会；权利 例句 (vt. 进入) You need a password to access the company's database. 你需要密码才能进入公司的数据库。 (n. 机会) Students need access to good libraries for their studies. 学生们需要有机会使用良好的图书馆来学习。 (n. 通道) The only access to the village is a narrow mountain road. 通往那个村庄的唯一通道是一条狭窄的山路。

# 68. excise

释义 vt. 切除，割去；删去 例句 (vt. 切除) The surgeon had to excise the tumor from the patient's lung. 外科医生不得不从病人的肺部切除肿瘤。 (vt. 删去) The editor decided to excise several unnecessary paragraphs from the article. 编辑决定从文章中删去几个不必要的段落。

# 69. rampant

释义 a.（犯罪、疾病等）猖獗的，肆虐的，泛滥的；（植物）蔓生的，疯长的 例句 (a. 猖獗的) Corruption was rampant in the government at that time. 当时政府中的腐败问题十分猖獗。 (a. 疯长的) The weeds were rampant in the untended garden. 无人照管的花园里杂草疯长。

# 70. narrate

释义 v. 叙述（describe, recount） 【派】narrative; narrator 例句 (v. 叙述) She narrated the story of her adventure in the Amazon rainforest. 她叙述了她在亚马逊雨林的冒险故事。 (v. 叙述) The documentary was narrated by a famous actor. 这部纪录片由一位著名演员叙述旁白。

# 71. amenity

释义 n. 生活设施；舒适 例句 (n. 生活设施) The hotel offers a range of amenities including a swimming pool and a gym. 这家酒店提供一系列生活设施，包括游泳池和健身房。 (n. 舒适) The amenity of the new apartment made it very attractive to young professionals. 新公寓的舒适性使其对年轻专业人士很有吸引力。

# 73. vigor

释义 n. 精力，活力 【派】vigorous 例句 (n. 精力) Despite his age, he still works with great vigor. 尽管年事已高，他仍然精力充沛地工作。 (n. 活力) The young team played with vigor and enthusiasm. 这支年轻的队伍充满活力和热情地比赛。

# 74. allot

释义 v. 分配；拨给；指派 【派】allocation; allocate 例句 (v. 分配) The manager decided to allot more resources to the new project. 经理决定为新项目分配更多资源。 (v. 拨给) Each student was allotted a desk and a locker. 每个学生被分到了一张桌子和一个储物柜。

# 75. flourish

释义 v. 繁荣；茂盛；兴旺；健康幸福 n.（为引起注意的）夸张动作；修饰 例句 (v. 繁荣) The local economy flourished after the new shopping mall was built. 新的购物中心建成后，当地经济繁荣起来。 (v. 茂盛) The plants flourished in the warm and sunny climate. 植物在温暖阳光充足的气候中茂盛生长。

# 76. diversity

释义 n. 多样性；差异 例句 (n. 多样性) The diversity of cultures in the city makes it a fascinating place to live. 城市文化的多样性使其成为一个迷人的居住地。 (n. 差异) There is a great diversity of opinions on this issue. 在这个问题上存在着很大的意见差异。

# 77. homotherm

释义 n. 恒温动物 例句 (n. 恒温动物) Humans are homotherms, meaning our body temperature stays relatively constant. 人类是恒温动物，这意味着我们的体温保持相对恒定。 (n. 恒温动物) Birds and mammals are homotherms that can maintain a stable internal temperature. 鸟类和哺乳动物是能够保持稳定内部温度的恒温动物。

# 78. attend

释义 v. 出席，参加；随同，陪同；专心，注意 例句 (v. 出席) The president will attend the international conference next week. 总统将出席下周的国际会议。 (v. 陪同) The prince was attended by several bodyguards. 王子由几名保镖陪同。 (v. 注意) Please attend carefully to what the teacher is saying. 请仔细注意老师说的话。

# 79. deceptive

释义 a. 虚伪的；骗人的（deceitful, misleading） 例句 (a. 骗人的) The advertisement was deceptive and led many customers to make wrong purchases. 这则广告是骗人的，导致许多顾客做出了错误的购买。 (a. 虚伪的) His deceptive smile hid his true intentions. 他虚伪的微笑掩盖了他真实的意图。

# 80. recession

释义 n.（经济的）衰退，衰退时期；撤回，退回 例句 (n. 经济衰退) The global recession caused many businesses to close down. 全球经济衰退导致许多企业倒闭。 (n. 撤回) The recession of the floodwaters took several weeks. 洪水的消退花了几周时间。

# 81. gush

释义 vi. 涌出（effuse） 例句 (vi. 涌出) Water gushed from the broken pipe in the basement. 水从地下室的破裂水管中涌出。 (vi. 涌出) Tears gushed from her eyes when she heard the sad news. 当她听到这个悲伤的消息时，眼泪涌了出来。

# 82. implicit

释义 a. 暗含的；含蓄的 例句 (a. 暗含的) There is an implicit agreement between the two parties. 双方之间有一个暗含的协议。 (a. 含蓄的) Her implicit criticism of the plan was not well received. 她对计划含蓄的批评并没有得到很好的接受。

# 83. accustomed

释义 a. 通常的；习惯的（habitual, conventional）；按照风俗习惯的 例句 (a. 习惯的) She is accustomed to waking up early every morning. 她习惯每天早上早起。 (a. 通常的) He arrived at his accustomed time of ten o'clock. 他在他通常的十点钟到达。

# 84. veil

释义 n. 面纱；遮蔽物 vt. 蒙着面纱；掩饰 例句 (n. 面纱) The bride wore a long white veil over her face. 新娘戴着一条长长的白色面纱遮住脸。 (vt. 掩饰) She tried to veil her disappointment with a smile. 她试图用微笑掩饰她的失望。

# 85. harsh

释义 a. 严厉的，严酷的；粗糙的，毛糙的；刻薄的；（天气或生活环境）恶劣的；刺耳的，刺目的 例句 (a. 严厉的) The teacher gave a harsh punishment to the student who cheated. 老师对作弊的学生给予了严厉的惩罚。 (a. 恶劣的) The harsh weather conditions made the climb extremely difficult. 恶劣的天气条件使攀登极其困难。 (a. 刺耳的) The alarm had a harsh sound that woke everyone up. 闹钟发出刺耳的声音，把所有人都吵醒了。

# 86. attitude

释义 n. 态度，看法；姿势 例句 (n. 态度) His positive attitude towards challenges inspired the whole team. 他对挑战的积极态度激励了整个团队。 (n. 姿势) She stood in an attitude of defiance with her arms crossed. 她双臂交叉，摆出蔑视的姿势。

# 87. imperial

释义 a. 帝国的；皇帝的 例句 (a. 帝国的) The imperial army marched through the capital city. 帝国军队穿过首都。 (a. 皇帝的) The imperial palace was the residence of the emperor. 皇宫是皇帝的住所。

# 88. host

释义 n. 主人；东道主；主办方；大量，许多 v. 主办；招待 例句 (n. 东道主) The host city of the Olympic Games prepares for years. 奥运会的主办城市要准备多年。 (v. 主办) The university will host an international conference next month. 该大学将于下个月主办一场国际会议。

# 89. algebra

释义 n. 代数 例句 (n. 代数) Many students find algebra difficult to understand at first. 许多学生起初觉得代数难以理解。 (n. 代数) He learned basic algebra before entering high school. 他在进入高中之前学过基础代数。

# 90. sculpture

#释义 n. 雕刻，雕塑；雕塑品 #例句 (n. 雕塑品) The museum has a beautiful sculpture of a horse made entirely of marble. 博物馆里有一尊完全由大理石制成的精美马雕塑。 (n. 雕刻) She studied sculpture at the art academy for five years. 她在艺术学院学习了五年的雕刻。

# 91. technical

#释义 a. 技术的，工艺的；专门性的，专业的 #例句 (a. 技术的) The engineer provided technical support to the construction team. 工程师为施工团队提供了技术支持。 (a. 专门性的) This report is too technical for ordinary readers to understand. 这份报告太专业了，普通读者难以理解。

# 92. inquire

#释义 v. 询问，打听；调查，查问 #例句 (v. 询问) She called the hotel to inquire about the availability of rooms. 她打电话给酒店询问是否有空房。 (v. 调查) The committee was set up to inquire into the cause of the accident. 成立了一个委员会来调查事故的原因。

# 93. export

#释义 v. 出口，输出 n. 出口；出口商品 #例句 (v. 出口) The country exports a large quantity of oil to other nations. 该国向其他国家出口大量石油。 (n. 出口商品) Rice is one of the main exports of this region. 大米是该地区主要的出口商品之一。

# 94. missile

#释义 n. 导弹；投射物 #例句 (n. 导弹) The military successfully tested a new type of missile. 军方成功测试了一种新型导弹。 (n. 投射物) The crowd threw missiles such as bottles and stones at the police. 人群向警察投掷瓶子和石块等投射物。

# 95. improvise

#释义 v. 即兴创作；临时凑合，临时准备 #例句 (v. 即兴创作) The pianist forgot her music and had to improvise during the performance. 钢琴家忘了乐谱，不得不在演出中即兴演奏。 (v. 临时凑合) We didn't have any tools, so we had to improvise with whatever was available. 我们没有任何工具，所以不得不用手边能找到的东西临时凑合。

# 96. infer

#释义 v. 推断，推论；推测 #例句 (v. 推断) From his silence, we can infer that he does not agree with the plan. 从他的沉默中，我们可以推断他不同意这个计划。 (v. 推测) What can we infer from the data collected during the experiment? 从实验期间收集的数据中我们能推测出什么？

# 97. candid

#释义 a. 坦率的，直率的；公正的，客观的 #例句 (a. 坦率的) She gave a candid opinion about the company's financial problems. 她对公司的财务问题给出了坦率的意见。 (a. 公正的) The journalist's candid report presented both sides of the argument. 这位记者客观公正的报道呈现了争论的双方观点。

# 98. shatter

#释义 v. （使）粉碎，（使）破碎；摧毁，破灭 #例句 (v. 粉碎) The glass fell to the floor and shattered into a thousand pieces. 玻璃掉到地上摔得粉碎。 (v. 破灭) The bad news shattered all her hopes of winning the competition. 坏消息破灭了她赢得比赛的所有希望。
