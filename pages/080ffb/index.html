Target now acknowledges the costume should have never been for sale saying,

"As a company, we got this wrong and we are deeply sorry.

We know this is especially hurtful for our black guests, team members, and partners.

Removing the costume is an important first step and we are looking closely at how this happened."

WE'RE CUTTING UP CARDS TODAY.

It comes 1 year after the company's controversial move to roll back its diversity, equity, and inclusion policies, which sparked protests and

boycotts by shoppers.

We need to hold larger corporations accountable.

One Minneapolis area business leader claims this new costume controversy is an indirect result of Target's DEI decision last year saying,

"You rolled back diversity, so who's in the room now to say, 'Hey, this is wrong.'

" One commenter on social media saying, "

The amount of people this costume had to go through to get approved says a lot.

What year is it?"

Target declined to comment on who designed and approved the product, only saying that it's looking at what needs

to change.

# 1. costume

  • 释义:
    • n. 服装；戏装；民族服饰
    • v. 为……提供服装
  • 例句:
    • n. The children wore Halloween costumes to the party. （孩子们穿着万圣节服装去参加派对。）
    • n. The museum displayed traditional costumes from various countries. （博物馆展出了来自不同国家的传统服饰。）

# 2. criticized

  • 释义:
    • v. 批评；批判；指责（criticize的过去式/过去分词）
  • 例句:
    • v. The politician was criticized for his controversial remarks. （这位政治家因其有争议的言论而受到批评。）
    • v. The movie was widely criticized by film critics. （这部电影受到了影评人的广泛批评。）

# 3. racist

  • 释义:
    • adj. 种族主义的；种族歧视的
    • n. 种族主义者
  • 例句:
    • adj. His racist comments sparked outrage on social media. （他的种族主义言论在社交媒体上引发了愤怒。）
    • n. He was accused of being a racist by his colleagues. （他被同事们指责为种族主义者。）

# 4. caricatures

  • 释义:
    • n. 漫画；讽刺画；夸张的描述（caricature的复数）
    • v. 用漫画表现；讽刺（caricature的第三人称单数）
  • 例句:
    • n. The artist drew caricatures of famous politicians. （这位画家画了著名政治家的漫画像。）
    • v. The comedian caricatures celebrities in his shows. （这位喜剧演员在演出中夸张模仿名人。）

# 5. evoking

  • 释义:
    • v. 唤起；引发；引起（情感、记忆等）（evoke的现在分词）
  • 例句:
    • v. The old song is evoking memories of her childhood. （这首老歌正在唤起她对童年的回忆。）
    • v. The smell of fresh bread was evoking a feeling of comfort. （新鲜面包的气味引发了一种舒适感。）

# 6. blackface

  • 释义:
    • n. 涂黑面孔（指白人演员将脸涂黑扮演黑人，被视为种族歧视行为）
  • 例句:
    • n. The use of blackface in performances is widely condemned as racist. （在表演中使用涂黑面孔被广泛谴责为种族主义行为。）

# 7. diversity

  • 释义:
    • n. 多样性；多元化；差异
  • 例句:
    • n. The company is committed to promoting diversity in the workplace. （该公司致力于促进工作场所的多元化。）
    • n. Cultural diversity enriches our society. （文化多样性丰富了我们的社会。）

# 8. equity

  • 释义:
    • n. 公平；公正；股本；资产净值
  • 例句:
    • n. The organization fights for social equity and justice. （该组织为社会公平和正义而奋斗。）
    • n. He owns 20% of the company's equity. （他拥有该公司20%的股权。）

# 9. policies

  • 释义:
    • n. 政策；方针；保险单（policy的复数）
  • 例句:
    • n. The government has introduced new environmental policies. （政府出台了新的环境政策。）
    • n. You should review your insurance policies annually. （你应该每年审查你的保险单。）

# 10. sparked

  • 释义:
    • v. 引发；触发；激起（spark的过去式/过去分词）
    • adj. 被激起的；被引发的
  • 例句:
    • v. The controversial decision sparked widespread protests. （这个有争议的决定引发了广泛的抗议。）
    • v. Her speech sparked a lively debate among the audience. （她的演讲在观众中激起了热烈的讨论。）

# 11. protests

  • 释义:
    • n. 抗议；反对（protest的复数）
    • v. 抗议；反对（protest的第三人称单数）
  • 例句:
    • n. Thousands joined the protests against the new law. （数千人参加了反对新法律的抗议活动。）
    • v. The students protests the university's decision to raise tuition. （学生们抗议大学提高学费的决定。）

# 12. boycotts

  • 释义:
    • n. 抵制；联合拒绝购买（boycott的复数）
    • v. 抵制；拒绝参加（boycott的第三人称单数）
  • 例句:
    • n. Consumers organized boycotts of products from certain countries. （消费者组织了针对某些国家产品的抵制活动。）
    • v. The union boycotts companies that treat workers unfairly. （工会抵制不公平对待工人的公司。）

# 13. accountable

  • 释义:
    • adj. 负责任的；有责任的；应作解释的
  • 例句:
    • adj. Managers must be held accountable for their decisions. （管理人员必须为他们的决定负责。）
    • adj. The government should be accountable to the public. （政府应对公众负责。）

# 14. Minneapolis

  • 释义:
    • n. 明尼阿波利斯（美国明尼苏达州最大城市）
  • 例句:
    • n. Minneapolis is known for its lakes and cold winters. （明尼阿波利斯以其湖泊和寒冷的冬天而闻名。）
    • n. She moved to Minneapolis for a new job. （她为了新工作搬到了明尼阿波利斯。）

# 15. indirect

  • 释义:
    • adj. 间接的；迂回的；不直截了当的
  • 例句:
    • adj. There was an indirect reference to the scandal in his speech. （他的演讲中间接提到了那桩丑闻。）
    • adj. The bad weather had an indirect impact on sales. （恶劣天气对销售产生了间接影响。）

# 16. declined

  • 释义:
    • v. 下降；减少；衰落；婉拒（decline的过去式/过去分词）
    • adj. 下降的；衰退的
  • 例句:
    • v. The number of visitors declined significantly last year. （去年游客数量大幅下降。）
    • v. She politely declined his invitation to dinner. （她礼貌地婉拒了他的晚餐邀请。）
