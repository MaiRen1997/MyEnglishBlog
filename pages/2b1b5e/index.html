**1. break-in**
  • 释义:
    • n. 闯入；非法侵入（通常指盗窃）
  • 例句:
    • The police are investigating a break-in at the local jewelry store.

2. incident

  • 释义:
    • n. 事件；事故（通常指不寻常或令人不悦的事）
    • adj. 入射的（物理术语，较少用）；易发生的（古语）
  • 例句:
    • n. Nobody was hurt in the incident, but it was very frightening.

3. devastated

  • 释义:
    • adj. 极度震惊的；心碎的；被彻底摧毁的
    • v. (动词过去式) 毁坏；使震惊 (devastate 的过去式)
  • 例句:
    • adj. She was absolutely devastated when she lost her wedding ring.

4. facility

  • 释义:
    • n. 设施；设备（常用复数）；天赋；才能；场所
  • 例句:
    • The new sports facility has a swimming pool and a gym.

5. puzzling

  • 释义:
    • adj. 令人困惑的；费解的
    • v. (动词现在分词) 使迷惑 (puzzle 的现在分词)
  • 例句:
    • adj. I find his strange behavior very puzzling.

6. approximately

  • 释义:
    • adv. 大约；大概
  • 例句:
    • There were approximately 50 people at the party.

7. barn

  • 释义:
    • n. 谷仓；牲口棚
  • 例句:
    • The farmer stores all his hay in the big red barn.

8. broken into (短语)

  • 释义:
    • v. (过去分词) 强行闯入 (break into 的过去分词)
    • adj. phr. 被强行打开的
  • 例句:
    • v. Our car was broken into last night, and the radio was stolen.

9. ventilation

  • 释义:
    • n. 通风；通风系统；空气流通
  • 例句:
    • The room has poor ventilation, so it feels very hot.

10. unimaginable

  • 释义:
    • adj. 不可想象的；难以置信的
  • 例句:
    • The amount of work they did was unimaginable.

11. declined

  • 释义:
    • v. (动词过去式) 下降；减少；衰退；婉拒 (decline 的过去式)
  • 例句:
    • v. She politely declined my invitation to dinner.

12. comment

  • 释义:
    • n. 评论；意见
    • v. 发表评论；评论
  • 例句:
    • n. He made a rude comment about my hair.
    • v. The boss refused to comment on the new project.

13. deferring

  • 释义:
    • v. (动词现在分词) 推迟；延期 (defer 的现在分词)；遵从 (defer to)
  • 例句:
    • v. They are deferring the decision until next week.

14. meat

  • 释义:
    • n. (食用的) 肉；肉类；(水果的) 果肉
  • 例句:
    • I don't eat red meat; I only eat fish and chicken.

15. revenue

  • 释义:
    • n. 收入；收益；税收（政府）
  • 例句:
    • The company's annual revenue increased by 10% this year.

16. cruelty

  • 释义:
    • n. 残忍；残酷；虐待
  • 例句:
    • The animal shelter was set up to stop the cruelty to stray dogs.

17. tampered

  • 释义:
    • v. (动词过去式) 擅自改动；破坏；篡改 (tamper 的过去式)
  • 例句:
    • v. Someone has tampered with the brakes of this car.

18. triggered

  • 释义:
    • v. (动词过去式) 引发；触发（尤指一系列事件或反应）(trigger 的过去式)
  • 例句:
    • v. The loud noise triggered the car alarm.

19. spike

  • 释义:
    • n. 尖刺；钉子；猛增；峰值
    • v. 猛增；急剧上升；加烈酒于
  • 例句:
    • n. There has been a sudden spike in electricity prices.
    • v. Electricity prices are expected to spike during the summer.

20. rare

  • 释义:
    • adj. 稀有的；罕见的；（肉）半熟的，三分熟的
  • 例句:
    • adj. It is very rare to see snow in this city. / I like my steak rare.

21. unheard

  • 释义:
    • adj. 未被听到的；未被注意的（通常与 of 连用表示“前所未闻的”）
  • 例句:
    • His cries for help went unheard. (Listen to the phrase: unheard of)

22. Throughout

  • 释义:
    • prep. 贯穿；遍及；在整个...期间
    • adv. 自始至终；到处
  • 例句:
    • prep. It rained throughout the entire day.

23. referring

  • 释义:
    • v. (动词现在分词) 提及；参考；求助于 (refer 的现在分词)
  • 例句:
    • v. I am referring to the document we discussed yesterday.

24. estimated

  • 释义:
    • v. (动词过去式) 估计 (estimate 的过去式)
    • adj. 估计的；预计的
  • 例句:
    • adj. The estimated cost of the building is five million dollars.

25. financial

  • 释义:
    • adj. 财政的；金融的；财务的
  • 例句:
    • The company is in good financial health.

26. poultry

  • 释义:
    • n. 家禽（鸡、鸭、鹅等）；家禽肉
  • 例句:
    • They raise poultry on their farm, mainly chickens.

27. wholesaler

  • 释义:
    • n. 批发商
  • 例句:
    • The restaurant buys its vegetables from a local wholesaler.

28. ruled out (短语)

  • 释义:
    • v. (过去式) 排除；取消；使...不可能 (rule out 的过去式)
  • 例句:
    • The police have ruled out robbery as the motive for the crime.

29. purposefully

  • 释义:
    • adv. 有目的地；故意地；坚定地
  • 例句:
    • He walked purposefully towards the door to speak to the manager.

30. tampering

  • 释义:
    • n. 篡改；破坏
    • v. (动词现在分词) 擅自改动 (tamper 的现在分词)
  • 例句:
    • n. The police are investigating the tampering of the safety equipment.

31. stolen

  • 释义:
    • v. (动词过去分词) 偷窃 (steal 的过去分词)
    • adj. 被偷的
  • 例句:
    • adj. The police found the stolen car abandoned in the forest.

32. footage

  • 释义:
    • n. 连续镜头；（影片的）片段；以英尺计的长度
  • 例句:
    • The security camera captured clear footage of the thief.

33. anonymously

  • 释义:
    • adv. 匿名地；不具名地
  • 例句:
    • The donation was made anonymously, so we don't know who sent it.
